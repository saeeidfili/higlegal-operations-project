Calibration

Capacity μ ≈ 23.5 videos per month, fitted to the reported 3–7-day wait across demand 15–20 (bracket 22.0–23.7). Implied dedication 23.5 × 10 h ÷ 352 h ≈ 67%. At 100% of paid hours the ceiling would be 35.2.

Tail and check

P90 wait ≈ 10 days; about 1 in 5 lawyers waits more than a week (19%). A 200k-order simulation reproduces the formulas within 1.2%. Falsifiable: the inbox should hold about 2–5 requests.
