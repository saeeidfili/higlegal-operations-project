Our improvement strategy

Three iterations, one after the other. Each keeps what the previous one achieved.

ITERATION 1

Standardize

Structured briefs and approvals to reduce waiting and rework.

ITERATION 2

Automate scripting

AI-generated scripts with human review to reduce manual work.

ITERATION 3

Integrate production

Integrated video generation with human quality control to eliminate manual synchronization and format adaptation.

Standardize first, then automate, then integrate.
