How to read it

One illustrative order through the 12 steps, 18.8 calendar days end to end. Red bars are waiting, black bars are active work.

About 10 h of staff effort in total (company-reported). Bars show calendar spans, so work bars look longer than the effort inside them.

Note on step 11

A/V sync is the largest manual activity, not necessarily the capacity bottleneck. Capacity depends on total work per order plus rework.

Illustration, not a measured order history.

Source: illustrative trace from our process analysis; lead time 2–4 weeks and ~10 h effort reported by the HiLegal team

Backup for questions. MAIN MESSAGE: the order spends most of its 18.8 days waiting: 5 days in the intake queue, about 3 days of clarification that is mostly waiting, 2 days each at script approval and final approval. Active work is spread thinly in between. DATA STATUS: illustrative trace (Lawyer Chen example), not measured; spans are calendar days and do not measure value-added time. Work of 10 h over 2–4 calendar weeks is 1.5–3.0% of wall-clock hours or about 6–13% of an 8-hour working-day clock; neither is a value-added PCE. Step 8 (final script) has no own span in this trace. The chart labels AV sync as bottleneck; on our slides we call it the largest manual block, because the longest task is not automatically the capacity bottleneck. SOURCE: queue model folder, project repository Section 4 (queue), chart by our team.
