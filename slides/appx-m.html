Queue model (M/M/1, intake queue only)

Capacity μ ≈ 23.5 per month, calibrated to the reported 3–7-day wait at 15–20 requests per month. Poisson arrivals, steady state. Approval waits come on top.

Process simulation (12 steps, all versions)

Per-task minutes are our split of the reported ~10 h. Loop chance per gate in the baseline 0.75 / 0.20 / 0.40. 36-month runs, 3 seeds, 20 requests per month. Rework does not rise with congestion, so results are conservative.

Two capacity budgets, never mixed

KPI math: 2 × 160 h × 60% ≈ 11,500 min per month ≈ 19 videos. Simulation: 67% dedication ≈ 14,150 min per month.

Validation and limits

The baseline reproduces the reported 15–20 videos per month and the 3–7-day wait. One company, partly self-reported data, no controlled test: a pilot replaces the estimates.
