SPEAKER: Saeed Filizadeh. TIME: 1:15. MAIN MESSAGE: Rework looks like extra work but is mostly extra waiting. Removing it would cut the lead time roughly in half while the work falls only by about 14%. SPEAKING POINTS: (1) Same team, same demand, with and without the three rework loops: work rises by only 84 minutes per video, lead time by about 11 days. The queue cost of rework is about 29 times its work cost. (2) The chart: left, mean lead time per scenario with the P90 whisker (1 in 10 orders); right, hands-on work per video against the company-reported ~10 h. Halving rework already saves about a week. (3) Rework also lowers capacity: about 27 videos a month without rework, 24 at baseline, 21 with heavy rework. Push demand to 22 a month and the heavy-rework process is past capacity: the backlog keeps growing. (4) The ceiling is a property of the team plus its rework. Kill the loops, not the overtime.
