MAIN MESSAGE: The headroom is worth something concrete: scale without hiring, a typical customer who flips from −¥1,160 to +¥360 a month, about ¥270k a year of payroll freed for the core, and a process that feeds the legal LLM. Operations stops taxing the strategy and starts compounding it. SPEAKING POINTS: (1) The design held up under stress, so the process now has headroom. What is it worth? Four answers, each with its condition on the slide in gold. (2) Capacity: same two producers, ~19 → ~190 videos a month. If demand grows above 19 a month, the paid service scales to every tier without hiring; video stops being the reason to say no. (3) Cost: ¥940 → ¥180 per video. If the typical customer uses about 2 of their 10 videos, that customer flips from −¥1,160 to +¥360 a month, and the yearly loss of ¥150–195k goes to about break-even, before any revenue growth. (4) Human time: 10 h → 1 h per video. That frees about ¥270k a year of production payroll, if management explicitly redirects it to the core; absorbed as overhead, the gain disappears. (5) Flywheel: from Iteration 2, every edit and change request is labelled training data, so the video process improves the legal LLM. Strategy: the moat is maintained by a pipeline, and the pipeline generates fine-tuning data.
