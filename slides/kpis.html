TIME: 1:00. MAIN MESSAGE: We define the six measures before showing solutions and distinguish company-reported figures from our estimates. SPEAKING POINTS: (1) Read the six tiles left to right, top to bottom. Each tile shows the KPI, its baseline and a short definition. Icons identify the measures and the numbered circles preserve their order. Company-reported labels mean HiLegal supplied an approximate figure, not that we independently measured it. Estimate labels identify our modelling assumptions, not measured pilot outcomes. (2) Lead time is request received to delivery of the finished video, including all waiting and rework. The reported range is 14–28 calendar days, equivalent to 2–4 calendar weeks. Use calendar days consistently when comparing that baseline. Staff processing time is active human work per delivered order, about 10 hours as reported by the company. (3) Cost is an estimate: 10 hours × ¥85/h estimated loaded rate + ¥90 estimated tool costs = ¥940 per video. HiLegal must validate the loaded rate and tool fees. (4) Throughput is completed output, reported at 15–20 videos per month. Production Capacity is a separate model metric: maximum sustainable output. The company-stated ceiling is 35–40 per month; about 19 at 60% effective use is a model estimate. Higher capacity produces higher throughput only when demand exists. (5) Rework Rate counts the share of completed orders with at least one return at Clarification, Approval or Final Approval. The ~90% baseline is our estimate, anchored in the reported repeated brief revisions. Revision rounds count individual corrections; one order with four rounds counts once in Rework Rate. (6) Quality Acceptance Rate follows the main-branch master-data definition: share accepted first-pass at every gate, with zero rework loops. Under this definition ~10% is the complement of the estimated ~90% rework rate, not an independently measured internal-QC result. It is not a claim that only 10% of delivered videos are legally accurate or acceptable. Quality criteria and the logging of first-pass outcomes must be agreed with HiLegal and checked in the pilot. DATA STATUS: Reported baseline estimates exist for lead time, human processing time and throughput. Cost, rework and first-pass acceptance currently use model estimates, not observed company baselines. The pilot must replace estimates with observations. KPI 6 was added to the original proposal’s five measures to track first-pass quality. TARGETS (not results): proposal targets include lead time of 1–2 business days, human processing time under 1 hour and roughly tenfold capacity. Calendar-day and business-day targets must be distinguished. See the scenarios slide and Appendix A for the reconciliation.
