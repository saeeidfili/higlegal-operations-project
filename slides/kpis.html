TIME: 1:00. MAIN MESSAGE: We fix the yardstick before showing any solution, and we are explicit about which baselines exist and which are gaps. SPEAKING POINTS: (1) KPIs 1–5 come from our proposal; KPI 6, Quality Acceptance Rate, is added so that "maintaining quality" in the central question can be checked. (2) Data status: three baselines are company-reported estimates; Cost per Video, Rework Rate and Quality Acceptance Rate have no baseline yet. Measuring them is the first task of the pilot. (3) Throughput is what was actually completed in a month. Production Capacity is what the process could sustain; the reported ceiling is 35–40 per month. Higher capacity becomes higher throughput only if demand exists. (4) Rework Rate counts orders, revision rounds count corrections. One order with four brief rounds counts once in the Rework Rate and four times in revision rounds. DEFINITIONS TO AGREE WITH HILEGAL: Lead Time unit = business days (the baseline is reported in calendar weeks: 14–28 calendar days ≈ 10–20 business days). Rework = any return at Clarification, Approval or Final Approval; denominator = orders completed in the period. Quality Acceptance Rate: criteria (e.g. factual and legal accuracy of the script, correct lawyer details, audio and lip-sync quality, format compliance) to be agreed with HiLegal; stage = internal QC before the lawyer's final approval; denominator = videos assessed at that stage. Cost per Video: loaded hourly rate and tool fees to be provided by HiLegal. TARGETS (proposal §4, NOT results): Lead Time 1–2 business days; Human Processing Time under 1 h; roughly tenfold capacity.
