Finite-horizon results

All WIP values are means over a finite 36-month run. Heavy rework at 20 / month has incomplete observations; at 22 / month the system is overloaded. These points are valid run values but do not describe a stable system.

Modelled capacity: 27.4 (no rework), 23.6 (baseline), 20.6 (heavy) per month.

Backup for questions. MAIN MESSAGE: the capacity wall is a property of the team plus its rework. With no rework, 22 requests per month are absorbed easily (WIP about 8.9); with baseline rework WIP rises to about 20; with heavy rework the process passes its capacity of 20.6 per month and the backlog grows without limit. Values (mean WIP): no rework 5.2 / 6.2 / 7.6 / 8.9, baseline 10.3 / 12.7 / 15.3 / 20.4, heavy 14.9 / 16.8 / 35.3 (censored) / 81.0 (overload) at 15 / 17.5 / 20 / 22 per month. Overloaded and censored points have no stationary mean lead time; do not quote them as averages. The chart labels heavy at 22 as overload; heavy at 20 is plotted like a normal point but is censored in the reconciled table. Figures match the reconciled results on main (7 Nov 2026).
