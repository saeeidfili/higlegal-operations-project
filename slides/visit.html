Business

Cross-border legal-tech platform operated by FaDaDa, a Shenzhen-based e-signature provider. Core asset: its fine-tuned legal LLM.

Customers

Chinese companies expanding abroad, matched with the international lawyers listed on the platform.

Our focus: social media videos for lawyers

HiLegal produces promotional videos for lawyers’ social media, used to advertise and build visibility in the Chinese market.

  • Bundled with the $100/month tier, which promises 10 videos
  • Made with AI tools (ChatGPT, Seedance, TTS), ~15–20 per month
  • Scaling it depends on the process we improve

Our analysis draws on discussions with the HiLegal team and company-provided process information. The process map was confirmed with HiLegal in September 2026.
