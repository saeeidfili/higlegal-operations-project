TIME: 1:15. MAIN MESSAGE: The first change needs no new technology: standardize what enters the process and how lawyers approve, so fewer orders travel the rework loops back through the queue. CHANGED STEPS (thick red), in the confirmed As-Is order: the Video Request (1) becomes a structured request form with format previews; the Intake Queue (2) is removed because complete requests auto-route; an automatic completeness check replaces Clarification (3) and bounces incomplete requests back to the form; the Content Brief (4) is auto-filled from the form and the lawyer's profile; Approval (7) and Final Approval (12a) use a structured link with a 48-hour SLA and automatic reminders. Unchanged: script drafting, AI refinement and all production steps (5, 6, 8 to 12b). Rectangles are activities, the triangle is the removed queue, diamonds are decisions. MECHANISM → KPI: (1) Lead Time: the lawyer picks the format before anything is produced, so fewer briefs fail Clarification; each avoided loop avoids another pass through the 3–7-day queue. Structured approvals with an SLA cap the wait at each gate. Target ~21 d → ~9 d (7–12 d). (2) Rework Rate: fewer orders returned at Clarification. (3) Human time 10 h → 7 h 15 target (intake 60→15 min, brief 50→10, fewer redo rounds); cost ¥940 → ¥705 target (labour only at ¥85/h estimate; tools unchanged). (4) Capacity ~19 → ~26/month calculated (11,500 productive min ÷ 435 min); throughput rises only with demand. (5) Rework ~90% → ~55%, QAR ~10% → ~45% (targets). Standardization is also the basis for later Kaizen steps (lecture Session_1.2 slide 13: "stabilize first, then raise the bar"; Session-5 5S: Standardize). EVIDENCE AND ASSUMPTIONS: ≥4 brief rounds and the loop back to the intake queue are company-reported (proposal Table 1, Fig. 1). Plausibility (Appendix A): if each failed clarification re-waits the full queue, three extra loops cost 9–21 days. Pilot gate (10 videos): median lead ≤ 12 d, 0 clarification loops on ≥ 8/10, human time ≤ 8 h, rework ≤ 60%, QAR ≥ 35%, no quality drop. TRADE-OFFS: keep a route for non-standard requests; lawyers may still iterate heavily (proposal §5 risk) — mitigated by previews and structured change requests. REMAINING LIMITATION: ~6 h 40 of the 7 h 15 is the untouched manual middle (scripting, generation, sync, adaptation). STATUS: proposed design; not tested. All iteration figures are targets.
