SPEAKER: Augustin Dulon. TIME: 0:50. MAIN MESSAGE: The "AI video service" is mostly people. One video costs about ¥940 to make, HiLegal books ¥117 against it: the cost model the service was sold on is off by a factor of eight. SPEAKING POINTS: (1) Start with the producer, not the AI: salary ¥10,000 plus social insurance and housing fund ¥3,500 plus equipment and overhead ¥1,500 gives ¥15,000 a month fully loaded; over 176 productive hours that is about ¥85 per hour (our build-up). (2) Each video takes about 10 hours of staff work (company-reported), so ¥850, plus ¥90 of model and tool credits: about ¥940 per video. Labour is about 91% of it; the AI is the cheap part. (3) HiLegal's own attribution is ¥117 per video, model credits plus a 30% overhead buffer (company-reported). Against ¥940 that is off by eight times. (4) HiLegal told us the team works around the clock and people keep resigning: output only grows with headcount. Bridge: next, what this means per customer.
