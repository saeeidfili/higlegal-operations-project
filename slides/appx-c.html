Appendix C | Rework simulation

Rework adds little work but a lot of waiting

Modelled, not measured: all four scenarios from one common 3-seed simulation run at 17.5 requests per month (reconciled on main, 7 Nov 2026). Heavy rework is a stress scenario.

C

Backup for questions. MAIN MESSAGE: in the model, rework changes lead time far more than work. No rework vs baseline: work 516 vs 600 min (+84 min), mean lead time 10.9 vs 21.6 calendar days. Reduced rework: 551 min, 14.9 days; heavy rework: 686 min, 30.7 days. Whiskers are P90 (1 in 10 orders): 12.9, 21.6, 33.7, 54.1 days. Rework share of videos with at least one loop: 0%, 66%, 87%, 93%. DATA STATUS: loop probabilities are estimates fitted to the reported at least four brief rounds and ~10 h of work; lead time is not fitted and lands inside the reported 14–28 days. Per-task minutes are our decomposition. CONSISTENCY: all values come from the common 3-seed experiment (reconciled results table on main, 7 Nov 2026); they are not mixed with the earlier single-seed scenario runs (11.0 / 21.8 days). Baseline minus no rework: +84 min of work, +10.65 days of lead time; the older headline "+10.8 days" comes from the single-seed run. SOURCE: rework simulation, project repository Section 4 (Rework), chart by our team.
