## Speaker notes

**TIME:** 1:00. Land the 8× contrast by 0:50; slide 4 (the bleed) starts at 1:30.

**MAIN MESSAGE:** The "AI video service" is 91% people. One video costs ≈ ¥940 to make — HiLegal books ¥117 against it. The cost model the service was sold on is off by 8×.

**SPEAKING POINTS:**

1. Start with the producer, not the AI: salary ¥10,000 + insurance & housing fund ¥3,500 + equipment and overhead ¥1,500 → ¥15,000/month fully loaded. Over 176 productive hours, that is ≈ ¥85/h. *(our build-up [E])*
2. Each video takes ~10 h of human labour [C] → ¥850. Add ¥90 of model/tool credits and the total is ≈ ¥940 per video — labour is 90.6% of it. The "AI" in the service is the cheap part.
3. HiLegal's own attribution assumed ¥117/video — model credits plus a 30% overhead buffer [C]. Against ≈ ¥940 actual, that is off by 8×.
4. Bridge to next slide: every video is −¥823 — next we show what that does per customer.

**DATA STATUS:** Producer cost build-up, ¥85/h rate, and ≈ ¥940 total are our estimates [E] (Shenzhen-market figures, sanity-checked). The ~10 h per video and the ¥117/video attribution are company-reported [C]. Full build-up, cost ladder, and per-customer bleed tables in appendix A1.
