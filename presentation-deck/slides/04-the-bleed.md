## Speaker notes

**TIME:** 1:00 — starts 2:15 (Dedi takes over), ends 3:15. Land the table in ~40 s, drain + thesis in ~20 s.

**MAIN MESSAGE:** The $100 tier is priced below the cost of delivering it — the loss starts on the first video, and the yearly burn is eating the resources that should build the moat.

**SPEAKING POINTS:**

- The most popular tier is $100/month ≈ ¥720 [C], and it promises 10 videos.
- We just saw one video costs ≈ ¥940 [E] to make. So look at the table: a customer who uses **one** video already puts us −¥220 in the hole.
- The typical customer uses 2 — that's −¥1,160 a month. A customer who claims the full entitlement costs us −¥8,680 a month.
- Break-even is 0.77 videos/month: there is no usage level where this tier makes money. The incentive is inverted — the more customers use what they paid for, the more we lose.
- Scale check: the team produces 15–20 videos/month total — enough to fully serve about two customers. Every heavy user we acquire deepens the bleed.
- And it compounds: ¥150–195k a year burned, plus engineering time spent propping up video — while the actual moat, the legal LLM, is starved.
- We are not arguing for cheaper videos at any cost. Thesis: automate and optimize the process — AI-aided QC for speed — more videos, same quality, less money.
- Bridge: to fix it we have to see it — next, the current process as it actually runs.
