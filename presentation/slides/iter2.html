TIME: 1:15. MAIN MESSAGE: On top of the standardized inputs from Iteration 1, the AI writes the first draft from a fixed template and the lawyer's profile, so employees edit instead of write. Quality stays with a human edit and check before the lawyer sees it. CHANGED STEPS: thick red = new in Iteration 2: "AI Draft + Human Edit" replaces manual Script Drafting. "AI Refinement" is removed because refinement now happens inside the drafting step, before approval (today it happens after approval). Thin red = changes kept from Iteration 1. MECHANISM → KPI: (1) Human Processing Time: drafting becomes editing. The saving is real in direction but we cannot size it: the baseline time for manual drafting is not measured. We leave a labelled gap instead of inventing a number. (2) Capacity rises by the same unknown amount, because staff hours are the constraint. (3) Cost: lower labour, plus an AI tool fee; net effect unknown until both are measured. (4) Lead Time: small gain; drafting is not where orders wait. (5) Rework Rate: could fall (template formats match what the lawyer chose) or rise (AI errors caught at approval) — to be tested. (6) Quality Acceptance Rate: main risk of this iteration; the internal QC step is where it is measured. INCREMENTAL VS CUMULATIVE: vs Iteration 1 the change is in staff time; vs baseline it adds to the fewer loops of Iteration 1. COMPARE IN THE PILOT: time for AI draft plus human edit vs manual drafting plus AI refinement, including review time and content quality (briefing, iteration evaluation table). TRADE-OFFS: AI-generated content may need extra corrections (proposal §5 risk) → human QC; vendor pricing and lock-in → dual-vendor evaluation. REMAINING LIMITATION: separate audio and video plus 2–3 h of manual sync. STATUS: proposed; not tested.
